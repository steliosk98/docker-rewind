# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a small utility for backing up containers, not a cause worth
being unpleasant to a stranger over.

## Expected

- Assume good faith. Most conflict here is a misread message, not malice.
- Criticise code, not people. "This drops errors on the restore path" is review.
  "Did you even test this?" is not.
- Accept that the answer to a feature request is often no. The scope charter in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) is not a personal judgement of your idea.
- Respect that maintainers and contributors are volunteers with jobs, time zones,
  and lives. Nobody owes you a review by Tuesday.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Persistently re-litigating a closed decision, or brigading an issue thread
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, and reporting vulnerabilities publicly
  to force a response

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, and any space where you
represent this project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainer by email or GitHub private message. Reports stay
confidential and get a response within a week.

Responses, roughly in order: a private word, a public correction, a temporary
block, a permanent ban. Which one depends on severity and whether it is a
pattern. The maintainer decides, and the goal is a project people want to
contribute to — not a tribunal.

If you are the one being reported and you think it's wrong, say so once, clearly.
It will be considered honestly.
